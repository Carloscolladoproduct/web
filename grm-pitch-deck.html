01 · The problem

Hotels are leaving their biggest
margin pool to chance.

Growth

Room rates are plateauing

In mature markets, ancillary and non-room revenue is the fastest-growing margin pool. Hotels measure it as TRevPAR — and manage it with spreadsheets and gut.

Guests

Guests get spam, not relevance

Static campaigns: fixed price, fixed audience, fixed moment. During-stay upsell conversion sits at 1.7%. Relevance is the product nobody ships.

Us

Hotels pay rivals to use our data

Revinate at ~€400/mo, Cendyn at ~€750/mo — CRM tools built on delayed data pulled out of Mews. We supply the raw material; they capture the margin.

A decisioning layer.
Not another campaign tool.

Reach

GRM sits on top of the guest profile and continuously decides what to offer, to whom, when, through which channel, at what price — then measures the incremental revenue it created. Maximizing guest lifetime value the way the RMS maximizes the room night.
